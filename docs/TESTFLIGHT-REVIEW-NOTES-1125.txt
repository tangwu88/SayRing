Say Ring 1.0.5 (1125), cn.saydian.ring, team W7SXQ4A226, iPhone.

This beta updates the light activity/sleep interface, QRing partial-history retention and seven-day completion tracking, and restores ordinary LuckRing/CoolWear device settings. Compatible rings may expose incoming-call and message reminders, sedentary/drinking schedules, basic alarms and automatic-monitoring intervals only after actual SDK support is confirmed. HR and SpO2 share one hardware interval. Settings writes require device acknowledgement and fresh matching readback where supported. Some firmware cannot read its message-reminder switch: the UI explicitly reports an unknown original state and limits confirmed-command state to the current connection. Notifications use the iPhone's Bluetooth Share System Notifications permission and the phone's notification/DND settings; this is distinct from app push delivery.

CURRENT PUBLIC SCOPE
The same public product/platform configuration is used for users and reviewers. On 2026-10-22 the public iOS configuration enables read-only heart rate, HRV, blood oxygen, skin temperature and stress, in addition to activity/sleep. Manual heart rate, HRV and blood oxygen require the fixed manual SDK catalogue, fresh public lease and actual device capabilities. Skin temperature is not core body temperature and is not used to diagnose fever. The health encyclopedia and dedicated sleep-AI flag are enabled; mall visibility remains disabled. Sleep AI still requires its separate published authorization, service availability and explicit sleep-summary upload confirmation. Raw sleep-stage timelines are not sent by that report flow. No review-specific account, hidden switch or different server exists.

Clinical assessments/alerts, disease prediction, blood pressure, glucose, ECG and general health AI remain unavailable. Additional physiological acquisition does not expand cloud uploads; account activity/sleep and consent-controlled care retain existing boundaries. Device estimates and health trends are for daily wellness, not diagnosis or treatment. Consult a healthcare professional when needed. Privacy and terms: https://app.saydian.cn/say-ring/privacy and https://app.saydian.cn/say-ring/terms.

ACCESS AND VERIFICATION
Please retain and use the existing beta-review email/password credentials; email login is password login, not email OTP. No-login local ring use is also available. A compatible physical ring is needed for live records; phone sensors do not create ring data. No synthetic live health values are supplied.

Development-signed Profile on an actual iPhone XR/iOS18.7.10 with the owner's HR05 passed incoming-call write/readback/restoration, sedentary exact-schedule preservation, disabled temporary alarm add/delete/restoration, and shared monitoring interval 60-to-5-to-60-minute readback with other switches unchanged. Message enablement was explicitly requested and acknowledged. After restoring the normal Profile 1125, the tester additionally confirmed that real incoming calls and messages both triggered HR05 reminders. Timed sedentary/drinking/alarm triggers, full sampling-period accuracy and other ring models remain unverified. HR05 had no confirmed sleep sample in this test. Unconfirmed QRing calorie units remain unknown; we do not guess a conversion or delete SDK sleep segments to improve apparent accuracy. Source regression and physical-device evidence are recorded separately.
