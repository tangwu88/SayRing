Say Ring 1.0.2 (1101) updates the current QRing/LuckRing-compatible ring app. The iOS data views and account synchronization remain limited to steps, distance, activity calories and device-reported sleep duration/stages. Existing physiological records are retained but are not displayed or uploaded by this iOS version. Manual physiological measurements, clinical alerts, disease-risk estimates and device sleep scores remain unavailable for all iOS users.

Changes from 1.0.1:
1. Device > Gesture control: use only modes reported by the connected ring, read its actual state, save a selection and independently read it back. R21 on iPhone 15 Pro Max passed off/video/music/reading mode roundtrips and restoration of its original settings. The owner separately confirmed actual music control in a music player. Other ring models, actions and target apps have not all been physically verified. OS Bluetooth pairing and compatible firmware are required.
2. Sleep opens the latest valid available record first, then supports explicit date selection. A missing date remains empty; no samples or stages are fabricated.
3. Device > Health monitoring configures supported firmware auto-monitoring switches and intervals, including physiological sampling settings when the ring reports them. It does not add physiological data views or manual measurements to iOS. Actual switch/interval readback and restoration passed on the owned R21.
4. The dedicated sleep AI report is available to signed-in users only when the server enables it. It requires the published sleep-analysis consent and a separate explicit confirmation to upload the selected sleep summary to the configured third-party AI service. It can produce a non-clinical AI sleep reference score and routine suggestions; it is not a device or medical score. Raw sleep segments remain local. General health AI and disease-risk analysis remain unavailable. This feature is not hidden for review accounts; the same rules apply to every iOS user. We do not claim a new provider-generation acceptance test or clinical validation.
5. Updated Bluetooth candidate routing (including R1/R2 prefixes and R7C) and synchronization handling. Actual vendor handshake and capabilities, not a name alone, enable features.

Use the existing review credentials below for email PASSWORD login, not the one-time-code field. The Guest entry remains available without registration; guest data stays local. Compatible ring hardware and Bluetooth permission are required for live ring functions. Existing review credentials/contact details are unchanged.

The app and sleep reference reports are for everyday activity and sleep routines, not diagnosis or treatment. Wearable sleep stages are estimates. Please consult a doctor before making any medical decision. No medical regulatory clearance or measurement-accuracy study is claimed.
